About Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh is a beautiful state located in the north-western Himalayas of India.

The state is famous for its snow-covered mountains, beautiful valleys, forests, rivers, lakes and hill stations.
Shimla, Manali and Dharamshala are among its well-known tourist destinations.

Interesting Fact: Himachal Pradesh is popularly associated with the name "Land of Gods".

The state is an excellent destination for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

Note: Weather in mountainous areas can change quickly, so travellers should check current conditions before travelling.

Travel Tips

  • Carry warm clothes when travelling to high-altitude areas.
  • Check the weather before travelling.
  • Carry comfortable shoes for walking and trekking.
  • Keep your belongings safe.
  • Respect local culture and traditions.
  • Avoid littering in mountains and forests.
  • Follow safety instructions during adventure activities.
  • Do not stand close to the edge of cliffs or fast-flowing rivers.
